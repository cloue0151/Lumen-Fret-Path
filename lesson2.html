Guitar Strings and Finger Numbers

A guitar string is made from different materials that vibrate to produce musical notes when plucked or strummed. These strings are stretched across the guitar and create sound through vibration.

A standard guitar has six strings arranged from the thickest string to the thinnest string.

Guitar Strings

  • 6th String – Low E

    The thickest string and produces the lowest sound.

  • 5th String – A

    Produces a deep and warm tone.

  • 4th String – D

    Commonly used for rhythm and chords.

  • 3rd String – G

    Often used for melodies and lead playing.

  • 2nd String – B

    Produces a brighter and smoother sound.

  • 1st String – High E

    The thinnest string and produces the highest sound.

Finger Numbers

  • 1 – Index Finger
  • 2 – Middle Finger
  • 3 – Ring Finger
  • 4 – Pinky Finger
